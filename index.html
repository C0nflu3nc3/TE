ASSIGNMENT 5

A small experiment in choosing well.

This interactive prototype demonstrates a recommendation service for marketplaces. It uses four fictional moisturizers and fixed matching rules. The AI in our project name describes the proposed future service.

We filter by budget, texture, and fragrance preference, order eligible items by price, and show up to three. We never add an ineligible item to fill the list.

Our planned beta criterion is that at least 8 of 10 novice shoppers choose within three minutes and explain one matching reason and one limitation. No alpha or beta results are claimed here.

Feedback stays in this browser tab’s session storage. It is not sent to a research team. A session record can be downloaded after the walkthrough.
